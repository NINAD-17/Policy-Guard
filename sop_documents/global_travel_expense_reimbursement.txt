1. PURPOSE & OBJECTIVE
   This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs all company-funded business travel, 
   client entertainment, home office hardware stipends, and corporate expense 
   reimbursements. It ensures transparent fiscal management, tax compliance, and fair 
   allocation of company resources across PulseGuard Technologies Inc.

2. SCOPE
   This policy applies to all global full-time employees, managers, and contractors 
   incurring authorized business expenses on behalf of PulseGuard.

3. PRE-APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
   3.1. Domestic Travel
        - Must be approved in writing by the direct Department Manager at least 7 days 
          prior to booking.
   3.2. International Travel
        - Requires approval from the Department VP and Chief Financial Officer (CFO) 
          at least 21 days prior to departure.

4. EXPENSE CATEGORIES & LIMITS
   4.1. Airfare & Rail
        - Standard Economy / Coach class is required for all flights under 6 hours duration.
        - Premium Economy / Business class is permitted only for international flights exceeding 
          8 hours with prior VP approval.
   4.2. Lodging & Hotel
        - Maximum reimbursable room rate is $220 USD/night (Tier 1 cities like NYC, London, SF 
          cap at $300 USD/night).
   4.3. Daily Meals & Per Diem
        - Daily meal allowance (breakfast, lunch, dinner) is capped at $75 USD/day per employee.
        - Alcohol is reimbursable only during approved client business dinners with itemized receipts.
   4.4. Home Office & Productivity Stipend
        - Remote employees are entitled to a one-time $500 USD workstation setup stipend and 
          a $60 USD/month recurring internet/utility allowance.

5. SUBMISSION & REIMBURSEMENT TIMELINE
   5.1. Itemized Receipts Required
        - Credit card slips showing only total amounts are not acceptable. Legible, itemized 
          receipts showing date, vendor name, individual items, and taxes are mandatory.
   5.2. Submission Deadline
        - Expense reports must be submitted via the company Expense Portal (Navan / Expensify) 
          within 30 calendar days of incurring the expense.
        - Late submissions beyond 60 days will be permanently denied.
   5.3. Payment Cycle
        - Approved reports are processed in the following bi-weekly payroll direct deposit cycle.

6. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
   The following expenses are strictly personal and non-reimbursable:
   - Traffic or parking fines, airline seat upgrade fees for personal preference.
   - Hotel in-room mini-bar, pay-per-view movies, or personal grooming items.
   - Expenses for spouses, family members, or personal travel extensions.
