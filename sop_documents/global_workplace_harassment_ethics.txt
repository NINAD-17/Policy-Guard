1. PURPOSE & OBJECTIVE
   PulseGuard Technologies Inc. is committed to fostering a safe, respectful, inclusive, 
   and equitable workplace. This Standard Operating Procedure outlines the company's 
   zero-tolerance policy toward workplace harassment, discrimination, bullying, retaliation, 
   and unethical business practices, while providing confidential reporting channels.

2. SCOPE
   This procedure applies to all employees, contractors, executives, interns, and applicants 
   across all global office locations, remote workspaces, company-sponsored events, 
   virtual communication platforms (Slack, Zoom, Email), and business travel.

3. PROHIBITED CONDUCT
   3.1. Discrimination
        - Differential treatment, denial of opportunities, or hostile actions based on race, 
          gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or nationality.
   3.2. Harassment & Bullying
        - Unwelcome verbal, visual, or physical conduct creating an intimidating or offensive 
          environment (e.g. offensive jokes, derogatory remarks, microaggressions, stalking).
   3.3. Sexual Harassment
        - Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or conditioning employment 
          decisions on sexual compliance (Quid Pro Quo).
   3.4. Retaliation
        - Any adverse action taken against an individual for reporting a concern or participating 
          in an investigation is strictly illegal and subject to immediate termination.

4. ETHICAL STANDARDS & CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
   4.1. Financial Integrity & Gifts
        - Employees may not accept gifts, hospitality, or favors exceeding $50 USD in value 
          from vendors, clients, or partners without HR and Legal disclosure.
   4.2. Outside Business Activities
        - Side employment or consulting that competes with PulseGuard or utilizes company 
          assets is prohibited without prior written approval from the Chief Legal Officer.

5. REPORTING & INVESTIGATION PROCEDURES
   5.1. Reporting Channels
        - Employees can report incidents directly to their Manager, People Operations (HR), 
          or via the 24/7 Anonymous Whistleblower Hotline (ethics@policypulse.dev).
   5.2. Confidentiality & Non-Retaliation
        - Reports are treated with strict confidentiality. All allegations are investigated 
          within 5 business days by an independent HR and Legal review committee.
   5.3. Investigation Timeline
        - Preliminary interview within 48 hours of ticket creation.
        - Formal findings and corrective action report finalized within 14 calendar days.

6. DISCIPLINARY SANCTIONS
   Violations of this protocol will result in progressive disciplinary action up to and 
   including mandatory sensitivity training, formal written reprimand, role demotion, 
   immediate termination for cause, and legal prosecution where applicable.
